How Handicap Factors Work

Handicap Factor: A multiplier applied to raw scores to normalize them across bow classes.

Lower factors (< 1.0): Indicate easier bow classes where higher scores are expected.

Higher factors (> 1.0): Indicate harder bow classes where lower scores are typical.

Base class (AMHB): Typically has a factor of 1.0 and serves as the reference point.

Adjusted Score: Raw Score × Handicap Factor = Score used for tournament rankings.
