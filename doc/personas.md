## Persona: Tournament Organizer

**Name:** Alex Johnson  
**Age:** 45  
**Occupation:** Professional Event Manager  
**Background:** Alex has been organizing archery tournaments for over 10 years. With a keen eye for detail and a passion for archery, Alex ensures that every event runs smoothly and efficiently. Alex is tech-savvy and appreciates tools that streamline the management process.

**Goals:**
- To efficiently manage tournament logistics.
- To ensure fair competition through accurate handicap management.
- To communicate effectively with participants.

**Challenges:**
- Managing large volumes of participant data.
- Ensuring timely communication with all stakeholders.
- Keeping up with the latest technology in event management.

## Persona: Archer

**Name:** Jamie Lee  
**Age:** 28  
**Occupation:** Professional Archer  
**Background:** Jamie has been competing in archery tournaments since high school and has won several national titles. Jamie is dedicated to improving skills and values fair competition. Jamie uses technology to track performance and stay informed about upcoming events.

**Goals:**
- To compete in and win tournaments.
- To continuously improve archery skills.
- To stay informed about tournament schedules and results.

**Challenges:**
- Balancing training with competition schedules.
- Accessing timely information about tournaments.
- Managing travel and logistics for events.
